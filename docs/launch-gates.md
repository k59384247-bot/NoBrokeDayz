## Payment capability gap found 29 October 2026

Official source: https://docs.bachs.io/guides/refunds. NGN bank-transfer payments are not refundable through the documented Refund API. A payment supports only one refund; after a successful partial refund the remainder cannot be refunded later (failed refunds do not count). This blocks assuming repeated per-slot refunds against one funded multi-worker charge. Obtain a provider-supported route and owner-approved contract before the affected integration is frozen or live funding enabled. Website research does not satisfy written acceptance of conditional holding/custody, live capabilities or account-specific fees. See docs/bachs-payments-reference.md.
